\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
The top-\/level simulation manager. 

The \mbox{\hyperlink{classostk_1_1simulation_1_1_simulator}{Simulator}} holds an Environment and a collection of Satellites, providing methods to advance the simulation in time and manage satellites.
