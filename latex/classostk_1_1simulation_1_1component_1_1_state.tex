\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents the state of a component. 

A \mbox{\hyperlink{classostk_1_1simulation_1_1component_1_1_state}{State}} holds a Status (Undefined, Disabled, Idle, Busy, Error) and a verbosity flag.
