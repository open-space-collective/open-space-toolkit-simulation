\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A geometry associated with a component. 

Represents a 3D geometric shape (Composite) attached to a \mbox{\hyperlink{classostk_1_1simulation_1_1_component}{Component}}, enabling spatial queries such as intersection and containment checks against other geometries and celestial objects.
