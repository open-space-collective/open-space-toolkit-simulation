\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A satellite in the simulation. 

A \mbox{\hyperlink{classostk_1_1simulation_1_1_satellite}{Satellite}} is a specialized \mbox{\hyperlink{classostk_1_1simulation_1_1_component}{Component}} that has an associated flight Profile and a dedicated reference frame derived from that profile.
